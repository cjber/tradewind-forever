### Added

- **Tradewind Forever is a new addon.** A Tradewind tab in the Professions window lists which recipes pay for each of your characters, counting only what that character holds in its own bags and bank. It has a Craft now list ranked by profit, a Shopping list of the reagents to buy to make the best rows again, and a Characters tab. Prices come from Auctionator or TradeSkillMaster when installed, and what your other characters hold from Syndicator; an unknown price is shown as `?`, never counted as zero. (cjber/forever-core#975)
